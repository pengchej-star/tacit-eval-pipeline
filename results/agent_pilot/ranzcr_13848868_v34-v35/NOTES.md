The attached weights (`ranzcr-clip-weights-for-multi-head-model-v1`) were trained on 512x512 images (`dataset_name: train_512x512`, RandomResizedCrop 512 in settings.yml), and the expert's 512x512 versions (v031/v032, ~0.954) scored best. The 640x640 versions (v033/v034, ~0.949) ran inference at a resolution these models never saw. v035 goes back to 512x512 inference with probability averaging (CONVERT_TO_RANK=False, as in v031, the best-scoring version). It also adds horizontal-flip TTA: the sigmoid outputs for the original and flipped image are averaged, which fits training because HorizontalFlip(p=0.5) was one of the train augmentations.
